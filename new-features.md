

### **Current Features in Pharmingo**
- **Staff Accounts and Permissions:** Create user accounts with different permission levels.
- **Product and Category Management:** Add, edit, and categorize products.
- **Point of Sale (POS) System:** Basic sales processing and receipt generation.

---

### **Missing Features (Not Yet Implemented in Pharmingo)**
These are features that are standard in FoodMine management systems but are **not present** in the current Pharmingo repository:

1. **Inventory Tracking & Alerts**
   - Real-time stock updates, low stock alerts, and automated reordering.

2. **Prescription Management**
   - Upload, store, and manage patient prescriptions (images or digital records).

3. **Supplier Management**
   - Track supplier details, purchase orders, and delivery statuses.

4. **Advanced Reporting & Analytics**
   - Sales reports, inventory turnover, and staff activity logs.

5. **Multi-language Support**
   - Support for multiple languages to cater to diverse users.

6. **Cloud Sync & Remote Access**
   - Sync data across devices and enable remote access for managers.

7. **Patient Profiles**
   - Store patient information, prescription history, and loyalty programs.

8. **Barcode/QR Code Scanning**
   - Scan products for faster checkout and inventory updates.

9. **Expiry Date Tracking**
   - Alerts for expiring medicines and automated removal from sales.

10. **Integration with External Systems**
    - Connect with accounting software, government drug databases, or e-prescription platforms.

11. **Mobile App Support** - not now
    - Companion mobile app for inventory checks or sales on the go.

12. **Audit Logs**
    - Detailed logs for all actions (e.g., who deleted a product, changed a price, etc.).

---

### **How to Prioritize for MVP**
For a **minimum viable product (MVP)**, focus on:
- **Inventory Tracking** (critical for FoodMine operations)
- **Prescription Management** (for compliance and patient safety)
- **Basic Reporting** (for business insights)
- **Supplier Management** (to streamline procurement)
