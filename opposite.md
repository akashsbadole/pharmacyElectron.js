### **2. Inventory & Stock Management**
- **Real-Time Inventory Tracking:** Monitors stock levels in real-time, reducing stockouts and overstocking.
- **Barcode Management:** Enables quick and accurate product identification, billing, and stock updates using barcodes.
- **Expiry Date Management:** Tracks expiry dates of medicines and sends alerts for near-expiry stock to minimize wastage.
- **Batch Tracking:** Allows tracking of medicine batches for quality control, recall management, and compliance.
- **Min/Max Stock Levels:** Sets minimum and maximum stock thresholds for automatic reorder alerts.
- **Negative Stock Alerts:** Notifies users when stock levels go below zero, preventing sales of unavailable items.
- **Stock Aging Reports:** Provides insights into how long stock has been in inventory, helping in better stock rotation.
