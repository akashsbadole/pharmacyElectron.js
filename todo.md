### 🛒 Grocery / Retail Features
High-volume focus with weight handling and broad inventory.

- **Scale Integration**: Direct connection to scales for automatic weight capture and pricing (produce, deli, bulk); support in-counter or countertop scales.
- **Weight/Loose Units**: Sell by kg, grams, pieces; tare weight support for containers.
- **Barcode and PLU Support**: Fast scanning; Price Look-Up (PLU) for non-barcoded items.
- **Real-Time Inventory Updates**: Auto-deduct on sale; multi-location stock check.
- **Bulk and Random-Weight Items**: Handle deli meats, produce, grains accurately.
- **EBT/SNAP and Multi-Payment**: Support food stamps, various payments.
- **Low Stock and Reordering**: Automated alerts; purchase orders.
- **Shrink and Loss Prevention**: Reports for inventory discrepancies.
- **Customer Loyalty**: Points, discounts for regulars.(Basic features only)
- **Detailed Reporting**: Sales by department, peak times, profit margins.
