## Sales Process Optimization

### **Lead Qualification Framework**

#### Ideal Customer Profile:
- **Business Size**: 1-50 employees
- **Annual Revenue**: $100K - $10M
- **Industry**: Garment retail, fashion, apparel
- **Current Challenges**: Multiple systems, inventory issues, offline needs
- **Decision Maker**: Owner, manager, or IT director

#### Qualification Questions:
1. "How many locations do you operate?"
2. "What's your biggest inventory management challenge?"
3. "Do you need offline capability for your POS?"
4. "How do you currently manage customer loyalty?"
5. "What's your timeline for implementing a new system?"

### **Demo Strategy**

#### Demo Flow:
1. **Problem Identification**: Start with their specific challenges
2. **Solution Demonstration**: Show relevant features first
3. **ROI Calculation**: Calculate potential savings/gains
4. **Implementation Planning**: Discuss timeline and process
5. **Next Steps**: Clear call-to-action

#### Demo Scenarios:
- **Single Store**: Basic POS and inventory management
- **Multi-Location**: Real-time sync and centralized management
- **High Volume**: Fast transaction processing and reporting
- **Complex Inventory**: Size/color variants and seasonal management
