# FoodMine Enquiry Form - Google Forms Setup Guide

This guide will help you create the Google Form for your foodmine.com landing page enquiry system.

## 🎯 **Form Structure**

### **Form Title**: "FoodMine POS System - Business Enquiry"

### **Form Description**: 
"Get in touch with us to learn more about FoodMine POS System. We'll help you find the perfect solution for your restaurant, cafe, or hotel business."

## 📋 **Form Sections and Questions**

### **Section 1: Business Information**
**Question 1: Business Name**
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes
- Help text: "Enter your business name"

**Question 2: Contact Person**
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes
- Help text: "Your full name"

**Question 3: Email Address**
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes
- Validation: Email
- Help text: "We'll use this to contact you"

**Question 4: Phone Number**
- Type: Short answer
- Required: Yes
- Help text: "Include country code if international"

**Question 5: Business Type**
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Restaurant
  - Cafe
  - Hotel
  - Bar/Pub
  - Bakery
  - Fast Food
  - Other

### **Section 2: Current Setup**
**Question 6: Current POS System**
- Type: Short answer
- Required: No
- Help text: "If you're currently using any POS system"

**Question 7: Number of Locations**
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Single Location
  - 2-5 Locations
  - 6-10 Locations
  - 10+ Locations

**Question 8: Expected Daily Transactions**
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Less than 50
  - 50-100
  - 100-200
  - 200-500
  - 500+

### **Section 3: Requirements**
**Question 9: Special Requirements**
- Type: Paragraph
- Required: No
- Help text: "Any specific features or requirements you need?"

**Question 10: Demo Request**
- Type: Multiple choice
- Required: Yes
- Options:
  - Yes, I'd like a live demo
  - No, just send me more information
  - Maybe later

**Question 11: Additional Comments**
- Type: Paragraph
- Required: No
- Help text: "Any other information you'd like to share"

## 🔧 **Form Settings**

### **General Settings**:
- ✅ Collect email addresses
- ✅ Collect names
- ✅ Limit to 1 response per person
- ✅ Response validation: Email format for email field

### **Presentation Settings**:
- Theme: Professional blue/green (matching foodmine.com)
- Font: Roboto or Arial
- Background: White or light gradient

### **Confirmation Message**:
**After submitting**:
"Thank you for your enquiry! We'll get back to you within 24 hours. In the meantime, feel free to explore our features on foodmine.com."

### **Email Notifications**:
- ✅ Send confirmation email to respondent
- ✅ Send notification email to you (your business email)

## 🌐 **Embedding in Landing Page**

### **Get Embed Code**:
1. Open your Google Form
2. Click the three dots (⋮) in the top right
3. Select "Embed"
4. Copy the iframe code

### **Landing Page Integration**:
The landing page will include the Google Form using an iframe with custom styling to match the foodmine.com design.

## 📊 **Google Sheet Integration**

### **Automatic Data Collection**:
- All responses will automatically save to a Google Sheet
- Sheet name: "FoodMine Enquiries"
- Columns will match the form questions
- Timestamp automatically added

### **Sheet Setup**:
1. Google Form automatically creates the response sheet
2. Add filters for easy sorting
3. Set up email notifications for new responses
4. Optional: Add conditional formatting for priority responses
